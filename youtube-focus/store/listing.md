## Short description (≤ 132 characters, same as the manifest)

Hide Shorts, home recommendations, Up next, end screens and comments on YouTube. Settings apply instantly.

## Category

Productivity

## Detailed description

Watch what you came for, not what the algorithm lines up next.

YouTube Focus hides the parts of YouTube designed to keep you scrolling. Each one has its own
switch, and changes apply to open tabs instantly, without reloading:

• Shorts: shelves, the Shorts tab and sidebar entry, Shorts in search and subscriptions. A Shorts
  link you open anyway plays in the normal player.
• Home feed: the recommendations on the home page. Choose a calm page with links to Subscriptions
  and Watch later, or go straight to Subscriptions.
• Up next: the recommended videos next to the player.
• End screens: the cards and suggested videos over the end of a video, and the "Up next"
  autoplay card.
• Comments (off by default): the comment section under videos.

Need a break? "Pause 15 min" in the toolbar popup shows YouTube as usual for a quarter of an hour,
then focus comes back by itself.

Pro ($1.99 once; free during early access):
• Focus schedule: focus mode only on the days and hours you choose, including overnight ranges
  and any time zone.
• Channel allowlist: on videos from channels you trust, comments, Up next and end screens stay.
  Add a channel from the popup while watching it.
• Subscriptions-only mode: everything except your subscriptions and search is hidden.

Works on www.youtube.com and m.youtube.com. Search, your subscriptions, playlists, Watch later and
history are never hidden.

What it doesn't do: it doesn't turn off YouTube's autoplay setting (use the switch in the
player), doesn't block ads, and doesn't change anything on other websites.

Private by design: no account, no analytics, no network requests. Settings stay in your browser.

YouTube Focus is an independent project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed or sponsored by
YouTube or Google. YouTube is a trademark of Google LLC.

## Single purpose

Hide distracting parts of YouTube pages (Shorts, recommendations, end screens, comments) with
per-feature switches, a pause, and optional schedule and channel exceptions.

## Permission justifications

| Permission | Justification |
| --- | --- |
| `storage` | Saves the user's switches, the end time of a pause, the focus schedule and the channel allowlist locally. |
| Content script on `https://www.youtube.com/*` and `https://m.youtube.com/*` | Injects a stylesheet that hides the chosen page sections, marks the page type on the page's root element so the right rules apply, shows a small "home feed hidden" card, and follows YouTube's in-page navigation. It reads only the page URL and, on video pages, the link to the video's channel (for the allowlist). It runs on no other site. |

Remote code: none. All code is bundled in the package.

## Privacy practices (data usage disclosure)

- Personally identifiable information, health, financial or payment information, authentication
  information, personal communications, location, web history, user activity: **not collected**.
- Website content: on YouTube pages the extension reads the URL and, on video pages, the channel
  link, **locally**, to decide what to hide. Nothing is stored except the user's own allowlist
  entries, and nothing is transmitted.
- No network requests of any kind. No data is sold or transferred to third parties, used for
  purposes unrelated to the single purpose, or used for creditworthiness or lending.
