Focus mode

What to hide on YouTube. Changes apply to open tabs right away, no reload.

    Instead of the home feed

    Focus schedule PRO

    Focus mode is on only during these hours. Outside them, YouTube is shown as usual.

    Days
    An end time earlier than the start runs overnight (22:00 until 06:00 the next morning). The same time for both means all day.

    Channel allowlist PRO

    On videos from these channels, comments, Up next and end screens stay visible. Shorts and the home feed stay hidden. You can also allow a channel from the toolbar popup while watching one of its videos.

      No channels yet.

      Subscriptions-only mode PRO

      Hides everything except your subscriptions and search: the home page and Explore/Trending go to Subscriptions, and Shorts, Up next, end screens, comments and search suggestions are hidden.
