Video Speed+

Keyboard shortcuts

Single keys, used on any page with a video. They're ignored while you type in a field or hold Ctrl, Alt, Shift or ⌘. Keys are matched by their position, so they also work with non-Latin keyboard layouts.

    Speed

    Speeds go from 0.0625× to 16× (Chrome's limits).

    ×
    Per press of Slower / Faster
    ×
    Toggled with 1× by its key
    s
    Seconds per press

    Behavior

    On: each site keeps its own speed, and sites you haven't used yet start at 1×. Off: videos everywhere start at the last speed you used.
    Also apply to <audio> elements, e.g. podcast players. Off by default because many sites use them for sound effects.
    A small speed display in the top-left corner of a video, visible while you move the mouse over it. Its key shows or hides it on the current page either way.
    Remembered speeds

    Turned off on these sites

    Video Speed+ stays inactive there, including on subdomains and in players embedded in those sites.

      Privacy

      Video Speed+ runs entirely in your browser: no account, no analytics, no network requests. On the pages you visit it only looks at video and audio elements and at the keys you press (to match your shortcuts). Settings and remembered speeds are stored in this browser profile only.
