Central bank raises its key rate to 5.25%

By Anna Kowalski · March 3, 2024

By Anna Kowalski · March 3, 2024

The central bank raised its key rate by 25 basis points to 5.25% on Tuesday, its third increase this year. Governor Marek Nowak said inflation, at 6.1% in February, remained well above the 2% target.
