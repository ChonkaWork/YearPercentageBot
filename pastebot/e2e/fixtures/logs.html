Charges fail with 402 Payment Required

When a checkout fails, the payment service writes an entry like this to payments.log:

2026-09-27 14:03:12.481 ERROR [payments] ChargeFailedException: card_declined for anna.kowalski@example.com
  POST https://api.example.com/v1/charges?api_key=sk-proj-Xq7LmN2pR8sT4vW9yZ1aB3cD5eF6gH0jK2lM4nP9fQ2
  card=4242 4242 4242 4242 amount=129.00 EUR ip=203.0.113.42 phone=+1 415 555 0132
  Authorization: Bearer eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJzdWIiOiIxMjM0NTY3ODkwIiwibmFtZSI6IkpvaG4gRG9lIiwiaWF0IjoxNTE2MjM5MDIyfQ.SflKxwRJSMeKKF2QT4fwpMeJf36POk6yJV_adQssw5c
    at com.example.payments.StripeClient.charge(StripeClient.java:88)
    at com.example.checkout.CheckoutService.pay(CheckoutService.java:214)
    at /Users/anna/acme/checkout/server.js:120

Before you share a log in a support ticket, remove customer data and keys.

Common causes

  • The card was declined by the issuer (card_declined).
  • The API key belongs to a different account or environment.
  • The amount is below the minimum for the currency.
