The slope

Least squares picks the slope that minimises the squared errors:

β1=∑i=1n(xi−xˉ)(yi−yˉ)∑i=1n(xi−xˉ)2\beta_1 = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^{n} (x_i - \bar{x})(y_i - \bar{y})}{\sum_{i=1}^{n} (x_i - \bar{x})^2}β₁ = Σ (xᵢ − x̄)(yᵢ − ȳ) / Σ (xᵢ − x̄)²

Means

Both sums use the sample mean x̄ = (1/n) Σ xᵢx¯=1n∑i=1nxi, and the same for yy.

How good is the fit?

The coefficient of determination compares the leftover error with the total variation:

R² = 1 − SSres / SStotR2=1−SSresSStot

The errors are assumed to be normal, ε ~ 𝒩(0, σ²)ε∼N(0,σ2), with the same variance for every observation.
