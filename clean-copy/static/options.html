Cleanup

Fonts, colors, links and hidden text are always dropped: Clean Copy puts plain text on the clipboard.

Line breaks
Merging joins lines that were wrapped (PDFs, emails, fixed-width text). Blank lines, list items and table rows stay separate.
Off: - item becomes item. Numbered lists keep their numbers.
utm_source, fbclid, gclid and similar, in any address in the copied text.
Runs of spaces become one. Indentation and tabs are kept.
Links keep their address after the text: the guide (https://…).
Curly quotes become straight, … becomes ..., thin and other special spaces become normal spaces. Dashes – and — become - and  -  unless you keep them.
**bold**, # headings and * bullets become plain text, [text](url) becomes text (url). Code blocks are kept as they are.

        Privacy
        
          
        Clean Copy works entirely on your computer. It has no server, no account and no analytics, and it makes no network requests.
        
          
        A page is read only when you use Copy clean, the shortcut or the toolbar button on it, or when you copy on a site you added to auto-clean. The clipboard is read only when you click Clean clipboard or use its shortcut. Settings and rules are stored in this browser only.
        
          
        For Undo and Show changes, the last clean copy (its original text and formatting, and the clean text) is kept in memory only: the next copy replaces it, and it is gone when you close the browser. Forget in the toolbar popup removes it right away.
