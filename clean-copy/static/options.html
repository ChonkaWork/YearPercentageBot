        Privacy
        
          
        Clean Copy works entirely on your computer. It has no server, no account and no analytics, and it makes no network requests.
        
          
        A page is read only when you use Copy clean, the shortcut or the toolbar button on it, or when you copy on a site you added to auto-clean. Settings and rules are stored in this browser only.
