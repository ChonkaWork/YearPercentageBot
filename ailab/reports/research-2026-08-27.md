## Brazilian autonomous workers face a hard NFS-e electronic-invoicing deadline that can freeze their payments

**Проблема:** Brazilian municipalities (São Paulo, Curitiba, Florianópolis) are ending the old RPA (Recibo de Pagamento Autônomo) receipt system and requiring autonomous/freelance workers to issue a proper NFS-e (electronic service invoice) through the national issuer. In São Paulo the requirement is retroactive to January 1, 2026, with penalties for non-compliance starting August 1, 2026. Contracting companies need the NFS-e to claim tax credits under Brazil's tax reform, so an autonomous worker who doesn't comply risks having payments withheld/blocked by the businesses that hire them — a large population of freelancers (drivers, tutors, translators, technicians, etc.) who have never had to issue formal tax invoices before now need a way to do it correctly and on time.

## Freight brokers needing to monitor FMCSA bond balances in near real time or lose operating authority

**Проблема:** A new FMCSA Broker and Freight Forwarder Financial Responsibility rule requires brokers to maintain at least $75,000 in qualifying financial security (cash, irrevocable letters of credit, or US Treasury bonds) and, if a claim drops that balance below $75,000, FMCSA will suspend the broker's operating authority if it isn't replenished within just 7 calendar days (down from 30 days previously). This turns bond-balance tracking from an annual paperwork item into something brokers must monitor continuously, since a single unpaid claim payout can trigger a very short compliance clock with an existential penalty (loss of authority to operate).
- Джерело: https://www.fmcsa.dot.gov/registration/broker-and-freight-forwarder-financial-responsibility-rule-faqs (unknown) — «"FMCSA will suspend the operating authority of a broker or freight forwarder if the available financial security falls below $75,000 and is not replenished in seven calendar days" (paraphrase of FMCSA rule as summarized in trade coverage).»

  - url: https://landline.media/clock-counting-down-to-broker-rule-compliance-deadline/ | date: unknown | quote: Reports on the compliance deadline countdown for the broker financial responsibility rule.

- Чому ніша може бути порожня (гіпотеза): Guess — the population of licensed freight brokers who hold their own bond (as opposed to using a surety company that already tracks this) may be small enough that no one has built a dedicated monitoring tool yet, or surety/BMC-85 providers may already informally notify brokers when balances drop.

**Filter:**
