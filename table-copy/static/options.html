Excel expects semicolons where numbers are written with a decimal comma (Ukraine, Germany, France and many more). The default follows your browser's language. TSV always pastes into Excel and Google Sheets as cells.

Excel files PRO

1,234.5, -7 and 12% become numbers you can sum and sort, read the way the page's language writes them. Codes with leading zeros (007), long IDs, prices with a currency, dates and anything else stay text, exactly as on the page.
