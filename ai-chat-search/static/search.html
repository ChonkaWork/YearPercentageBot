      Search tips

      • Words match the start of a word: regex finds “regexes”.
      • All words must match. Quotes match an exact phrase: "sort by key".
      • Case and accents don't matter: cafe finds “Café”, львів finds “Львів”.
      • Tags are searchable too: a word matches a conversation's tags as well as its text.
      • Press / to jump to the search box.
